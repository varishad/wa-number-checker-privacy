Privacy Policy: WA Number Checker by ToolsACE

Last updated: 2026-10-23

WA Number Checker by ToolsACE is developed by toolsace.io.

What the extension does

You paste phone numbers into the side panel, or import them from a CSV or TXT file you choose. The extension asks WhatsApp Web, in a tab where you are already logged in, whether each number is registered on WhatsApp, and shows you the result. Imported files are read on your device only and are never uploaded.

Data we collect

None. The developer does not collect, receive, store on a server, sell or share any data. The extension has no analytics, no tracking and no remote servers of its own.

Data stored on your device

The extension keeps the following in your browser's local storage, on your device only:

  • the numbers you pasted and their results, and any numbers still waiting to be checked, so a paused run can be resumed when you reopen the panel;
  • a cache of recent YES/NO results (kept for 7 days), so a number you checked recently is not looked up again;
  • a daily lookup counter, used for the built-in safety limit.

CSV and Excel (XLSX) files are created on your device when you click Export; nothing is uploaded.

The extension is free. A daily safety limit (200 lookups) helps protect your WhatsApp account.

Use the Clear button to delete the numbers, results, queue and cache. Removing the extension deletes everything it stored.

Network activity

The only network activity is WhatsApp Web's own communication with WhatsApp's servers, triggered from your logged-in session when a lookup runs. The extension itself makes no other network requests.

Not affiliated

This is an unofficial tool. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by WhatsApp LLC or Meta Platforms, Inc. WhatsApp may restrict accounts that perform many lookups in a short time; use the Safe pace and keep lists small.
